Cubetable adapts selected 2014 SRD 5.1 equipment data and rules into software
calculations. The implementation is incomplete, includes product-specific
validation limits, and does not imply endorsement by Wizards of the Coast.

The J2.5 reference catalog is a structured adaptation of the 2014 SRD 5.1.
PDF typography and whitespace are normalized, stable IDs and metadata are
added, and reference entries remain separate from executable mechanics.
Source and verification details accompany each batch in
data/tacticalcombat/reference/catalog-v1.json and
docs/srd51-catalog-coverage.md.
